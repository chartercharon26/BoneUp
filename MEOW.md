BoneUp Meow (installed as **BoneUp 喵**) is the softer, cat-only special edition of BoneUp. Keep sitting and your cat gradually turns grey, slumps and becomes a pale bone cat. Get up, then click **STAND UP** to help it recover.

## Download

[**Download BoneUp Meow 1.3 for macOS**](https://github.com/chartercharon26/BoneUp/releases/download/v1.3.0-meow/BoneUp-Meow-1.3-macOS.zip) · [Release notes](https://github.com/chartercharon26/BoneUp/releases/tag/v1.3.0-meow)

This special edition is **macOS only**. For original BoneUp on macOS or Windows, see the [main downloads](README.md#download).

## What's included

- Nine chibi cats with distinct appearances and interactions.
- Recorded cat sounds, volume control and Focus mode.
- Sitting-to-bones animation, STAND UP recovery and WALK countdown.
- Workday scenes, adjustable schedules and Task Mode.
- English / Simplified Chinese menus and speech bubbles.
- Separate app, settings and login item from original BoneUp.

## Install and choose English

1. Extract the entire ZIP and open `BoneUp-Meow-1.3.dmg`.
2. Drag **BoneUp 喵** into **Applications** (the DMG shortcut may be labelled **应用程序**).
3. Open the app from Applications and look for **🐾** in the menu bar.
4. Choose **🐾 → 语言 / Language → English**.

On first launch the app enables opening at login and creates Desktop / Dock shortcuts. Disable **Open at login** from the paw menu if desired. The download is ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized; review macOS Privacy & Security if it is blocked.

## Meet the cats

| Cat | Click interaction |
| --- | --- |
| Tuxedo | Scratches the screen, leaving three pink claw marks. |
| Grey & White | Sings a little tune with floating musical notes. |
| Snow White | Blushes and sends you a few shy hearts. |
| Cream | Curls into a loaf and takes a purring nap. |
| Ginger | The default cat: pushes a cup off the desk. |
| Grey Tabby | Stretches as far as possible, then yawns. |
| Calico | Gets distracted by a ball of yarn. |
| Black Cat | Turns out the lights for a spooky surprise. |
| Siamese | Lets out an impressively loud meow. |

Switch with **🐾 → Desk cat → Skin**. Click the SIT time to set a 1–60-minute sitting interval; use the speaker button for volume and Focus mode. Workday schedules and Task Mode remain available from the paw menu.
